# Starlight Intelligence — AI Teammates Positioning (candidate v0.1)

Status: **candidate / human approval required**. This is a customer-facing expression of the existing [constitution](BRAND.md), not a replacement for its civilizational mission or four surface modes.

## Master line
**Your AI teammates, your way.**

## Supporting line
**Build an AI workforce around your work, your tools, and your decisions.**

## Why it works
- **Your**: agency, portability and explicit authority, not an unconditional claim of data ownership.
- **AI teammates**: task-oriented collaboration with named capabilities and limitations; never anthropomorphic claims of personhood.
- **Your way**: configurable models, permissions, tools, memory scopes and spending boundaries, only where implemented.

## Message hierarchy
1. Recognition: Your AI teammates, your way.
2. Explanation: Configure and coordinate AI teammates in one workspace.
3. Mechanism: Choose supported models and tools; scope access, memory and budgets; inspect outputs and handoffs.
4. Proof: Show real task artifacts, supported integrations, measured quality and execution traces.
5. Invitation: Start with one bounded mission.

## Evidence line
**Built on research. Refined through practice. Designed around you.** This is a proposed editorial line, not a validated scientific or product superiority claim. Use only with specific research citations, evaluation protocols or customer evidence nearby.

## Modes
- **Horizon**: extend human capability and community contribution; retain the existing institution-scale mission.
- **Operator**: emphasize permissions, mission ownership, evidence, intervention, cost and recovery.
- **Academy**: teach independent judgment and reusable skills, not blind delegation.
- **Cultural**: creative collaboration and character expression without conflating fiction and product reality.

## Examples
Homepage: "Your AI teammates, your way." / "Bring the models, tools and workflows you trust into a workspace built for coordinated work." / CTA: "Explore the workspace".
Agent setup: "Choose what this teammate can access." / "Review tools, memory scope and spending limit before enabling execution."
Mission completion: "Review the result, sources and remaining questions." Never label a task completed before the runtime confirms completion.
Developer API: "Build on Starlight's agent contracts." Only display public endpoints that are deployed and documented.

## Avoid
"Fully autonomous", "scientifically proven", "zero lock-in", "best models", "unlimited", "secure by default" and "guaranteed ROI" without exact scope and independently inspectable evidence.

## Acceptance tests
- Reader identifies the product category and their next action in five seconds (test with real participants).
- No claim describes an unshipped capability as live.
- Every product claim points to a verified implementation or is explicitly labeled planned.
- All copy respects the existing BRAND.md, NARRATIVE.md and product contracts.
- External tagline clearance and visual-identity approval remain human gates.
