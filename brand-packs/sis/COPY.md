# Starlight Intelligence editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for AI architects, technical founders, institutions, investors, and operators designing long-lived intelligence infrastructure. The reader should understand the architectural consequence and the next decision.

## Voice

- Curious, confident, warm, systemic, and benevolent; Operator/control copy remains precise and restrained.
- Move from consequence to architecture to decision.
- Use technical nouns with stable meanings.
- Label deployed systems, active experiments, proposals, and aspirations accurately.
- Support important claims with primary evidence, traces, evaluations, or working artifacts.

## Native language

Agent owner, memory boundary, trace, evaluation, provenance, control, handoff, operator, cost, latency, risk, evidence.

Celestial terms can organize information. They should never replace the system state being described.

## Avoid

- Science-fiction fog and AGI prophecy.
- Cosmic decoration presented as architecture.
- Institutional boilerplate.
- Claims about humanity without a concrete mechanism or source.
- Mythic Arcanea language.

## Examples

“Every agent needs an owner, a memory boundary, and a visible record of action.”

“This evaluation shows where the workflow failed, who owns the next decision, and which evidence is still missing.”

## Research Foundry and owned-work language — proposal, 4 Nov 2026

**Customer's progress, not platform inventory:** a meaningful question → inspected sources → a decision with uncertainty → an editable artifact → a measured improvement. The Research Foundry composes existing approved research/Capability Foundry systems; never advertise a second system or imply a new runtime is deployed.

- Horizon: "Bring the question you cannot leave alone. Follow the evidence. Build what matters."
- Operator: "The decision draft is saved. The source check is incomplete. No paid action has been dispatched."
- Academy: "Build a small working method. Trace the evidence. Then adapt it to your own project."
- Public promise (candidate): "Research you can inspect. Decisions you can defend. Work you can keep."

Use durable control language for agency, costs, ledger allowances, human approvals, tenant boundaries, interruptions and recovery. Show the difference between a source-backed finding, an independently accepted customer outcome and a proposal. Platform orchestration fees and third-party inference use have different units; explain them plainly before checkout. Queen may be attentive, candid and lightly mischievous, but she cannot fabricate a running team, successful experiment, price or permission.

Public copy never states "unlimited frontier intelligence", "fully autonomous 24/7 team" or "we are an official partner" without exact verified evidence. A credit award, startup program, public tutorial or experimental model is not revenue, customer traction or corporate endorsement. Use related accepted work: `brand-packs/sis/NARRATIVE.md`, `BRAND.md`, and the source-only proposal https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-ops/pull/471 .
