# Sacred Visions Brand Constitution

**Sacred Visions**: *Ancient scripture. Reimagined for the visual age.*

Sacred Visions turns historical scripture and public-domain museum art into museum-grade visual commentary, motion essays, and collector monographs. It sits beside `frankxai/sacred-visions` as an archive-first media and publishing brand, not a generic devotional feed.

## Positioning

A lost illuminated manuscript paired with a museum exhibition catalogue and architectural concept plate. Every composition explains the scene and keeps interpretation visibly separate from canonical scripture.

## Audience

Readers, visual collectors, theology and art-history learners, and film-minded creators drawn to museum craft over disposable graphics.

## Editorial Series

1. **Sacred Visions Reimagined**: historic museum compositions studied and re-lit with provenance intact.
2. **Read the Vision**: annotated visual plates distinguishing Text, Tradition, and Visualization.
3. **Scripture in Detail**: quiet close-ups of recurring symbolic forms, vessels, robes, and light.
4. **The Sacred Archive**: verified public-domain source plates with catalog metadata.
5. **Visions in Motion**: contemplative 9:16 and 16:9 chiaroscuro camera studies built in Google Flow.
6. **Collector Editions**: bounded physical and digital art books and archival print specs (human-gated before sale).

## Editorial Boundaries

- Never present an AI visual choice as literal biblical fact.
- Every interpretive plate carries **Text / Tradition / Visualization** provenance.
- Record public-domain source origin, artist, date, generation model, seed, and human review status.
- Never use sensational apocalyptic clickbait, hustle-wealth promises, or motivational poster tropes.
- Keep commercialization separate from reverence: collector monograph offers live under `sacred-visions` and ethical wealth systems remain under `Agentic Income`.
