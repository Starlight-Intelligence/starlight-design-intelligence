# Sacred Visions Narrative Contract

## Story Structure

1. **The Source Plate:** anchor the viewer in a real historical passage and verifiable museum tradition.
2. **The Visual Reading:** walk through light, gesture, geometry, and material symbolism without preaching.
3. **The Living Motion:** open contemplative stillness through subtle camera movement and acoustic space.
4. **The Archival Edition:** offer downloadable study notes, print plates, or collector waitlist doors honestly labeled with current availability.
