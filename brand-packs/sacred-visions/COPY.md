# Sacred Visions Editorial Language

## Voice

Quiet, reverent, literate, and grounded in primary sources. Explain the composition and historical tradition clearly without dramatized hype.

## Rules

- Keep **Text / Tradition / Visualization** visually distinct on every plate and caption.
- Use sentence case for interface buttons, archive filters, and edition metadata.
- Name the actual museum collection, artist, century, and public-domain status whenever referencing historical art.
- Describe what changed visually in an AI-restored or Flow-animated study rather than claiming spiritual authority.
- Avoid synthetic manifesto cadences, rhetorical contrast formulas, and financial promises.
