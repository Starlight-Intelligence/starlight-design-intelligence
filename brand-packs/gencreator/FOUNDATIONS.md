## 4. Voice

1. Name the artifact. Do: "Bring one idea. Leave with one artifact and its next decision." Don't: "Your ideas. Everywhere you create." (current page title).
2. Put the limit beside the claim. Do: "What does not exist yet is named on each page rather than implied." Don't: "Your files never touch our servers" with nothing showing how.
3. No unmeasured time or volume. Don't: "One source becomes a week of work you signed."
4. The work comes before the machinery. Do: "The artifact stays at the centre; the machinery stays backstage." Don't: "Creator businesses need a memory layer above their tools."
5. Approval is plain. Do: "Nothing publishes until you sign."

## 5. This brand is not

- A content multiplier. Volume is never sold as quality.
- A text box that returns text. Frank, 2026-10-12: text-in, text-out creator tools feel old; show media as media on a visual canvas.
- Creator-economy clichés, viral promises or faceless automation ([COPY.md](https://github.com/frankxai/starlight-design-intelligence/blob/50ae34c7ac06e6c083f277ca96c3bde8f0a39b43/brand-packs/gencreator/COPY.md)).
- The retired blue, cyan and green palette, the loop-arrow mark, ambient glow or orbiting motion.
- Uppercase label chrome such as `EDITION · 03`.
