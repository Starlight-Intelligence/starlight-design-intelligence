# GenCreator editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for independent creators, content teams, creative directors, and founders producing media with generative tools. The reader should know what to make, how to maintain quality, and what finished means.

## Voice

- Visual, energetic, practical, and playful.
- Lead with the artifact or use case.
- Name formats, tools, production choices, and quality thresholds.
- Use active verbs and short instructions.
- Explain consistency through character, palette, framing, sound, and sequence.

## Avoid

- Creator-economy clichés.
- Tool inventories without judgment.
- Viral promises and synthetic enthusiasm.
- Volume presented as quality.
- Faceless automation as the default aspiration.

## Examples

“Start with one strong scene. Cut it into a reel, a carousel, and the opening frame of the longer film.”

“Keep the character, palette, and camera language consistent across every generation.”

## Creator-owned products and research-to-edition loop — proposal, 4 Nov 2026

A useful GenCreator output is an edition a human can **review, edit, attribute, export, reopen and improve**. Make before/after creative decisions visible, not just generated output count. Describe the founder's raw research and authoring work before introducing model names or tools.

- Provisional headline: "Make your first edition. Make the next one better."
- Useful product copy: "Keep your sources, voice and editable project together."
- Proof line: "Every published asset has an owner, a reviewed source and a way to change it."

Free samples, paid templates, Creator Studio, Academy and subscriptions must name distinct jobs and real delivery rights. A toolkit is not a hosted agent; a Research Foundry briefing is not proof that an artifact shipped. Separate variable inference spend from creator-workflow software value. No "viral by tomorrow", unlimited generation, rights-free commercial downloads or fabricated influencer evidence.

Preserve approved editorial/visual identity, including any pinned EDITION LAB direction where reviewed. This is a draft for product and design review; do not silently rebrand the shipped experience.
