## Publication architecture
One canonical authored article; derivative content adapts for the channel without duplicating the indexable original.

| Editorial property | Recurring question | Required minimum evidence | Intended continuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Architect's Journal | What did we build, decide or change? | Implementation, trace, decision record or working artifact | Technical guide / relevant product |
| The People Building Tomorrow | Whose work deserves attention? | Consented interview or factual contributor profile, outbound original links | Community connection |
| The Future We Choose | What human choice does this technology change? | Source-linked synthesis and explicitly labelled personal judgment | Subscribe / discuss |
| Frontier Notes | What did a new model or lab result actually change? | Original official source, tested task where claimed, limitations | Reproduce / compare |
| The Independent Builder | Can an AI-enabled product model work sustainably? | Economics ledger, test design and observed outcomes where available | Agentic Income experiment |
| The Studio | What did we create with sound, imagery or story? | Original media, credits, rights, production notes | Listen / read / experience |

These are **candidate labels**. Check current route names and content owners before adopting them as public navigation or duplicating an existing series.

## Audience and conversion routing
Do not sell the entire portfolio to every visitor. Identify one reader situation:
- Founder/operator: constraint, decision, cost and a reproducible next move. Technical depth available behind the opening.
- Creator/musician: real artwork, creative process, controllable workflow, ownership of output.
- Researcher/AI practitioner: method, sources, reproducibility, evaluation and uncertainty.
- Collaborator/community builder: a person and useful shared work, with consent and clear participation.
- Private individual/friends/family: a personal relationship page belongs to the authorized personal surface, not the commercial site. Never expose private files publicly.

CTAs are specific human choices: **Inspect the build**, **Read the method**, **Try the template**, **Listen to the release**, **Meet the builder**, **Request an architecture review**. Match availability to the actual state; do not advertise unopened checkout or a nonexistent event.

## The voice: precise, warm, original
Use the pinned `COPY.md` for rules and the website's `lib/voice/frankx-voice.ts` for enforceable phrase checks. Do **not** copy banned-word dictionaries into new files.

| Overworked positioning | Better, context-bound wording |
| --- | --- |
| "We're pioneering the future of AI." | "I tested this architecture against three failure cases. Here is what held up." |
| "Join our elite ecosystem." | "A small room for builders who bring real work and useful questions." |
| "Our AI agents can generate passive income." | "This is a testable offer. These are its costs, failure points and measured orders." |
| "We partner with the frontier labs." | "I evaluate public releases from frontier labs and publish what I can reproduce." |
| "The ultimate creator transformation." | "A repeatable way to turn a rough idea into a finished, ownable asset." |
| "My companies are changing the world." | "These projects explore different sides of the same problem. Here is the one to start with." |

Prefer concrete nouns and verbs: **built, compared, published, measured, wrote, recorded, tested, priced, cited, spoke with, disagreed, revised, released**. Premium is depth, editorial selection, craft, discretion and excellent work; never synthetic scarcity or superlative claims.
