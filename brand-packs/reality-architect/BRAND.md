# Reality Architect Brand

Reality Architect is an open practice for architecting a life: imagine a desired scene, build a feasible bridge, and keep an honest record. The five-move systems method remains part of that practice.

## Promise

Imagine it. Build it. Witness it.

The first useful result is an authored scene, one chosen act and a portable artifact. When the bridge requires an AI system, find its first gap through See, Design, Build, Automate and Compound.

## Boundaries

- Meaning is the person's contemplative interpretation; mechanism names the action, system or studied pathway. Keep both labeled.
- Desired scenes, plans, reported actions and reviewed records remain distinct. Saving a plan does not establish that an action occurred.
- `reality.md` v0.2 retains Identity, Aims, Attention, State, Systems, Environment, Feedback and Guardrails. `soul.md` is the separate inner contract; agents propose edits and the person decides.
- Studio text stays in browser storage and added images in IndexedDB until the person chooses to export. Do not generalize this promise to opt-in waitlist requests or another portfolio product.
- No claim that thoughts, feelings or quantum effects directly control external events; no health, income, relationship or transformation guarantee and no blame for a missed result.
- Native apps, hosted personal storage, automatic cross-device sync and agent handoff views remain proposals unless their owning product proves them.
