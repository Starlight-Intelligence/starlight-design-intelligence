# Design an editorial experience

Choose an existing edition and one real editorial decision. Record the editor's input, the decision and the artifact another person can open. Load the [constitution](../../portfolio/frontier-experience-constitution.md) and [editorial articulation](../editorial-articulation/SKILL.md). The owning editor keeps the commissioning, revision and publishing systems.

Trace one claim from its original passage or timestamp through the working draft to its placement in the reader's edition. Make the source reachable where the editor decides. Preserve selection, reading position and unsaved work when moving between outline, source and preview. Make missing evidence visible at the affected claim. Provide keyboard access to the same decision.

Test with a corrected or withdrawn source. Check what survives reopening the edition and what a second editor can recover from the handoff. Distinguish browser-local persistence from shared state. Compare the editor's task and the reader's view at phone, tablet and desktop widths. Record one observable change in effort, such as navigation steps or the distance from claim to evidence.

Deliver the source-linked draft, the reader preview, the tested revision and any unresolved claims. Leave distribution with the owning workflow and its approval record.
