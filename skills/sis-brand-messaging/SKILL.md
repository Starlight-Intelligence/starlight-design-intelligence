# Starlight Intelligence Messaging

Read `brand-packs/sis/BRAND.md`, `brand-packs/sis/NARRATIVE.md`, `brand-packs/sis/TEAMMATES-POSITIONING.md` and `brand-packs/sis/messaging.contract.json` before generating customer-facing copy.

## Workflow
1. Identify surface mode: Horizon, Operator, Academy or Cultural.
2. Determine intended reader, job, available evidence and next action.
3. Draft concrete, comprehensible language. Preserve human agency and clear limitations.
4. For each factual product claim, record implementation evidence and state: verified live, preview, in development, vision or unknown.
5. Retrieve approved visual assets from the existing asset registry; do not generate substitute logos.
6. Review accessibility, terminology, provenance and the existing editorial quality gates.
7. Return copy, evidence links, claim status, unresolved approvals and a reviewer-ready handoff.

The tagline is a **candidate**. Do not silently promote it to an approved public identity. Do not claim scientific proof from an editorial research aspiration.

## Checks
- Can a customer identify what is offered, what is configurable and what remains their decision?
- Is the product's actual state clear?
- Is the source, owner and approval status of every visual asset known?
- Does this preserve the institution's broader mission and the selected mode?
