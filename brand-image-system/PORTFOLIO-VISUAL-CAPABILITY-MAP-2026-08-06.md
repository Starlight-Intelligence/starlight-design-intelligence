## Quality and release rules

1. No candidate is called premium or production-ready without its actual final export and intended surface.
2. Generated text and pseudo-UI never count as product proof; rebuild them as exact code, diagrams, or real captures.
3. New logos are vector systems before 3D/raster applications.
4. Character consistency begins with approved model sheets, silhouette, turnarounds, palette, props, expression sheets, and rights records.
5. A flagship surface requires the repository's design contract, rendered desktop/mobile evidence, accessibility/performance/claim checks, separate maker/verifier/approver, production commit/URL, and rollback proof.
6. The Observatory may report asset status and metrics but cannot approve, publish, or mutate a brand pack.
