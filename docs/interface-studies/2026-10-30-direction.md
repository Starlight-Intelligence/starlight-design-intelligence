Status: proposed design studies and implementation handoff. This is not a product launch, identity approval, native-app release or replacement of product architecture.

## Decision

Make the useful artifact the center of each product. Reality Architect earns the next visit through one scene, one chosen act and an honest return. Starlight earns trust through inspectable work and evidence. GenCreator turns sources into authored editions. Arcanea turns imagination into coherent world material. FrankX teaches through an exercise and the proof it leaves.

Use distinct brand systems and shared release discipline. Do not turn the portfolio into one visual costume or create a new backend, catalog or app for each screen.

## The product must become easier to enter

The current Reality Architect Studio presents seven views and three equally weighted starting doors before the first practice. That is a visible comprehension risk; it is not measured abandonment.

Recommend one first session: author an ordinary desired scene, name a present constraint, choose one feasible act, then save/export. The complete desk remains available through progressive disclosure. A returning person should find today's act before a long brand introduction.

Keep the conceptual order: authored desire → planned bridge/act → explicitly reported action → reviewed record. Demonstration records never enter personal history or its counts. A plan saved today is still a plan.

Marketing proof should show the actual exported artifact and the path to create it. Use one primary action and a representative example. Prices, member counts, efficacy, purchase results and automated availability stay absent until verified.

## Visual and interaction rules

Reality Architect keeps graphite, blueprint structure and dawn personal language. GenCreator keeps paper, ink and spot red. Starlight's lab operator profile uses its local ink/gold/cyan roles; Arcanea's mythic imagery must produce usable creator artifacts. FrankX selects editorial, product or soul mode deliberately.

Sentence case and normal tracking throughout. Glass is confined to navigation or a useful proof layer; reading surfaces stay solid. Approved vector source assets replace generated marks. Existing fonts need their existing rights and actual browser proof before release; a generated lookalike establishes neither.

Motion follows useful causality: opening a scene, moving from current to desired, confirming save, explaining a review state. The static experience is complete first. Pause/offscreen behavior and reduced-motion equivalence are required. Studio receives no background decorative animation.
