## Release decision

Prioritize Essential as the first paid offer. Professional and Command Center remain packaged comparison candidates. React distribution is implemented; Figma-compatible token projection does not establish a native Figma library, and Framer/Webflow require their own native implementation and review.

The exact admission gates and approval record are in `examples/sis-living-library/docs/FIRST-PRODUCT-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`. Availability is preview-only; scoped functionality and design are testable; buyer usefulness and sustainable demand remain unvalidated. No revenue, marketplace approval, full accessibility conformance or production readiness is inferred from the candidate packages.

Rollback: revert the bounded PR extensions and keep the existing closed-checkout state. No production migration, billing object or customer entitlement was changed.
