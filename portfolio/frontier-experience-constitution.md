# STΛRLIGHT Frontier Experience Constitution

This is the portfolio mandate for product interfaces, editorial experiences and multimedia. Apply it with the [portfolio experience standard](experience-standard.md), the owning product contract and the selected [brand pack](../brand-packs/). Record the exact design-kernel commit and source decisions at task start.

## Design the work

Start with a real practitioner, their job and one meaningful outcome. Trace inputs, decisions, intermediate artifacts, revisions, approvals, output and recovery. Reuse existing product routes, editors, media and workflows. Prove the core interaction with real or explicitly labeled representative inputs.

Choose spatial canvases, timelines, inspectors, contact sheets, layered editors, immersive editorial or other forms where they materially improve that job. Compose for professional information density, strong typography, accessibility and deliberate motion. Select original imagery, video, sound, diagrams, demos and portable exports where each earns its place. Verify responsive layout, keyboard operation, contrast, reduced motion and loading behavior.

Show agent state with inspectable inputs, sources, artifacts, versions, costs and outcomes. Every progress state and product claim requires evidence. Preserve inputs after failure and show a real recovery action.

## Resolve identity before use

Figma owns approved design masters, VIS owns approved asset identity and provenance, and the owning brand and IP stewards control identity selection and use. [Character retrieval](../playbooks/character-canon-retrieval.md) and the [brand production foundation](../playbooks/brand-production-foundation.md) define source resolution. Preserve current brand boundaries and Arcanea canon. Resolve the exact asset ID, approved edition or variant, bytes and hash, scoped rights and intended use before generation or publication. A folio, shortlist, experimental image or attractive derivative cannot grant approval. Missing or conflicting proof blocks the asset and leaves an explicit unresolved state. Approval to use an asset and authority to publish are separate decisions.

## Verify the result

Reject unverified identities, generic decorative dashboards, fake data or capabilities, dead controls, unsupported claims, poor responsive behavior, missing rights and absent independent review. Capture a real baseline and candidate on phone, tablet and desktop. Exercise the main workflow and relevant failure states. Record baseline, screenshots, intended visual changes, visual regression result, interaction, accessibility, performance and provenance evidence for the exact revision. Use separate design, technical and identity reviewers. Preserve stricter product, editorial, brand, accessibility and [web release](../evals/web-release-gate.md) requirements.

The [Frontier Experience Review](../evals/frontier-experience-review.md) requires at least 85/100 with every applicable critical check passing. A weighted score cannot clear an identity, functionality, provenance, accessibility or publication failure. Production authorization is a separate named human decision under the owning release system.
