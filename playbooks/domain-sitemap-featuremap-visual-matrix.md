# Portfolio Domain, URL, Sitemap, Featuremap, and Visual Funnel Matrix

This matrix maps every registered domain across the portfolio to its concrete routes, user transformation, entertainment and education funnel layers, Refero conversion archetype (`playbooks/refero-funnel-atlas.md`), and exact Google Flow / Antigravity / R2 visual slots.

Every route obeys `TRUTH.md` §2 and `evals/web-release-gate.md`: real artifacts before abstract slogans, honest availability (`DemandSignal` waitlist vs active Polar delivery), and static `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks for every video loop.

## 1. Entertainment and Education Funnel Architecture

Each flagship brand pairs **engagement depth** with **instructional clarity**:

| Funnel stage | Role | Visual and media format | Conversion bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1: Discovery & Entertainment** | Earn attention through world-building, story, sound, and beauty before pitching | `16:9` Horizon/Cinematic Veo/Omni hero loop (muted ambient + poster), `9:16` Reels/Shorts story cut, Suno music specimen | Single crisp narrative hook linking to the interactive workbench or guide |
| **Stage 2: Education & Proof** | Turn curiosity into mastery via inspectable mechanics, code, and provenance | Two-pane Pack Workbench (Archetype 2), Annotated **Text / Tradition / Visualization** plates, 5-slide `4:5` educational carousels | Live micro-diagnostic (`Archetype 4`) or real file excerpt preview |
| **Stage 3: Action & Retention** | Convert intent without pressure, track willingness-to-pay, and onboard cleanly | 4-step Fit-Check (`Archetype 1`), Numbered Edition Dossier (`Archetype 6`), Tier Ladder Desk (`Archetype 7`) | `DemandSignal` KV capture with price band or human-approved Polar delivery |

## 3. Per-Slot Delivery and Performance Contract

- **Web Hero Video (`16:9`)**: Max 8s seamless loop, muted autoplay with explicit `prefers-reduced-motion` toggle rendering the verified `.webp` poster frame (`<= 350 KB`).
- **OG / Share Card (`1200x630`)**: Clean raster plate at bottom layer + deterministic SVG/HTML sentence-case headline, brand mark, and URL slug at top layer.
- **Educational Carousels (`4:5` `1080x1350`)**: 5-slide structured teaching arc (Slide 1 Tension/Hook, Slide 2 Mechanism/Diagram, Slide 3 Real Proof/Artifact, Slide 4 Step Checklist, Slide 5 Honest Next Door).
- **Vertical Reels / Shorts (`9:16` `1080x1920`)**: 15% top/bottom safe zones reserved for platform UI; captions rendered by code/editor after video export.
