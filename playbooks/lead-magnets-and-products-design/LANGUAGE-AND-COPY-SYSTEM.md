All public copy in this file is engineered to pass `brand-packs/frankx/COPY.md` (First-120-Words Contract, Name-Swap Test, Builder-Taxonomy Test, Concrete-Anchor Test, Deletion Test) and `scripts/check-frankx-voice.mjs` with zero errors and zero warnings.

## 1. Five Editorial Laws for Digital Products and Funnels

1. **Name the concrete artifact in the first eight words.** Tell the reader whether they get a `.zip` repository kit, 6 Markdown checklists, a Node linter script, or a 200-prompt JSON catalog.
2. **Prove with file names, line counts, and time saved.** Replace abstract adjectives with inspectable facts: `6 Markdown playbooks`, `30 deterministic lint rules`, `15-minute terminal setup`.
3. **Keep public badges and buttons in sentence case.** Write `Free starter kit`, `Inspect 5 files`, `Get the blueprint ($67)`, and `Reserve edition 01 notice`. Never shout in `ALL CAPS`.
4. **Ban rhetorical crutches.** Do not use em-dashes, contrast formulas, earnings promises, fear framing, or filler verbs. State what the tool does, who it helps, and who should skip it.
5. **Pair every price with an explicit boundary.** State one-time vs monthly billing, license scope, and what requires human review before going live.
