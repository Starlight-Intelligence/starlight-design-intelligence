## Decision

Use **Evidence Desk** as the first cross-surface test. Its graphic behavior expresses what Starlight does: an interpretation stays attached to the source and the decision. Use Optical Bench to test Horizon imagery, Orbital Instrument for the Protocol's relationship views, and Observatory Editions for research and Academy publishing. The four are independent research hypotheses. Any eventual flagship release follows the existing three-direction review and founder selection. Review the four panels side by side before importing individual techniques.

Re-use the [SIS source authority](../brand-packs/sis/SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md), [surface modes and existing locks](../brand-packs/sis/REFERENCE-LOCKS.md), [November Horizon lock](../brand-packs/sis/REFERENCE-LOCK-SI-HOME-2026-11.md), [motion grammar](../brand-packs/sis/MOTION.md) and [Brand Image System](../brand-image-system/README.md). The brand kit owns the vectors. Figma serves as the review projection and the Living Design plugin remains the character-to-behavior lane. The October decision retains the four-point star, warm gold/cyan, Instrument type, observatory arrival and porcelain world. The Academy has its brighter treatment and the Protocol its orbit-star. Existing character approvals apply.

## Original design grammar

| Layer | Starlight rule | Working proof |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol and optical correction | Treat the canonical four-point silhouette as the invariant. Measure axis lengths, center mass, radii, tip acuity and geometric versus optical center. Keep original SVG bytes as the baseline. | Under controlled conditions compare the same original-scale star with and without the companion as an experimental visual control. Compare monochrome silhouette, optical centering and low-resolution separation. Assess the dot as a semantic companion, not an app-status cue. |
| Wordmark and type | Keep the source lockup and brand name in navigation. Instrument Sans carries product jobs, Instrument Serif carries human editorial emphasis, and IBM Plex Mono handles code and aligned data. | Test actual title/CTA, long product naming, wrapped 320/390px layouts, 200% zoom, fallback metrics and font provenance. |
| Color | Use the owning kit values. Ivory on ink and ink on paper carry meaning. Gold is warm emphasis on ink. Cyan is a bounded signal. The audit measured gold/paper 2.05:1 and cyan/paper 1.39:1. | Monochrome for small-size and light-paper uses per kit. Test text, focus, interactive boundaries and background-changing media against WCAG at render time. |
| Optical material | A solid reading plane carries content. A physically plausible medium needs a single lighting setup, surface thickness, roughness, occlusion and coherent reflections. Reserve live glass for controls where spatial hierarchy benefits. | Compare opaque and optical specimens. One bounded cinematic object, one light, light/dark fallback, reduced-transparency and high-contrast behavior. CSS blur is a simplification rather than physical refraction. |
| Orbit and space | Orbit encodes sequence, provenance or a real relationship. Put diagrams beside the evidence that explains them. Keep stable anchor and independently legible text. | Selected edge and its semantic label survive still frame, monochrome, reduced motion and mobile ordered list. Preserve the protocol mark variant. |
| Living motion | State, causality or editorial memory gives motion its purpose. Change one thing at a time while the readable shell holds. Keep the existing cursor behavior subject to its existing exemptions. | User-triggered start, pause or skip where applicable, interruptible control, equivalent static frame, no persistent idle activity in working surfaces. |
| Iconography | Use one visual family, optical weight and pixel-grid alignment for operators. State icons pair with text. Decorative diagrams use their own drawing layer. | 16/20/24px in browser, ink and paper, no reliance on tint, no repeated star as a generic AI action icon. |
| Portfolio | Share principles for evidence, accessible roles, provenance and source quality. SIS stays calm and precise. Academy is brighter, Protocol relational, FrankX authored, GenCreator work-led and Arcanea world-led. | Reuse semantic roles and evidence contracts. Each site keeps its owning type, mark, assets and approval workflow. |
| Editorial and film | A headline, one authored artifact and a precise caption carry the composition. Give every cinematic sequence an explicit still and a clear reading zone. | 16:9, 4:5, 9:16, cover crop and low-motion still. Use owned data, photographs and type layers. |

### Evidence order for a candidate prototype

1. Record kit path and hash. Export mono silhouettes and lockups first. Compare current master and any authorized historical controls supplied by the creative team.
2. Inspect equal-viewport, non-animated desktop and phone captures. Toggle ink/paper, motion and high contrast with all essential copy present. Use a reproducible fixture and note device/browser.
3. Record one trigger, duration, interruption and final state. Capture distinct ordered frames on desktop and mobile plus a time-separated stable reduced-motion sample. Measure resource and render cost on the representative lower-power device.
4. Attach actual source links, reviewer, Figma node and revision. For still comparison use same crop, exposure and zoom. For motion use synchronized frames and stable text. The output of the visual review is a candidate decision, with production treatment handled by the owning release process.

## Evaluation standards

Score 0 absent, 1 partial, 2 captured evidence. Twelve criteria, 24 points total. Candidate threshold 21/24 with each critical criterion at 2. Score evidence after seeing exports. A hypothesis name or Figma storyboard is insufficient evidence.

| Criterion | What earns a 2 |
|---|---|
| Identity authority (critical) | Exact owning source, source hash and approval state. Specimen and historical labels are clear. |
| Category distinction (critical) | Silhouette remains recognizable without tint or wordmark against Gemini and existing historical controls. Record conflations from at least five independent blind reads, randomize order, and report the count. Exploratory decision criterion: no more than one false attribution per five reads. |
| Small-size resilience (critical) | Current kit boundaries and stable contrast in 16/20/24/32px specimens. Record detached-point merging or disappearance. |
| Optical alignment | Balanced center across dark/light and name lockup with documented visual adjustments. |
| Typography | Real desktop and phone specimens with controlled reflow, documented licensed files and a working fallback. |
| Contrast and access (critical) | WCAG targets applicable to the surface, including 4.5:1 for ordinary text, 3:1 for large text and qualifying non-text controls, keyboard, reduced-motion equivalent and semantic labels. |
| Material | Lighting, reflection and occlusion logically agree. Opaque equivalent reads as well. |
| Motion | Trigger and job are evident. Mobile, interrupted and reduced-motion states preserve the meaning. |
| Causal product fit | A viewer can identify the source and the effect. Operator reveals are tied to real state or data. |
| Family fit | Lab, Academy and Protocol read as relatives without interfering with their different tasks. |
| Rights and provenance (critical) | Every input ties to its owner and permitted role. Third-party art remains outside production media. |
| Performance and maintenance | Device cold-load and input behavior meet the owning performance budget, with a tested fallback and a pinned compatible runtime. |
