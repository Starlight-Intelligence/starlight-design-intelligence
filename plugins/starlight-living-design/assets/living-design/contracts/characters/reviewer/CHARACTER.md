# Reviewer

Reviewer is a proposed visual identity for a design critic experience. Porcelain examiner is the form anchor.

## Identity boundaries
The character can represent a role in several authorized products. It is not a model, a live worker, a human professional or a canonical runtime identity. The existing Queen, Maker and Reviewer labels are preserved as role archetypes. New names are naming proposals.

Front, three-quarter, side and back; neutral and attentive poses; light and dark 32/64/256px avatars; static reduced-detail version; exact asset IDs and immutable renditions. Do not describe a family scene as an approved individual portrait.
