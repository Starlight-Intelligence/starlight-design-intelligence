# Aureon: visual contract

## Art direction
Use the Noble Machines parent. Preserve the ceramic humanoid steward silhouette. Keep one functional prop appropriate to systems steward; avoid ornament that competes with controls.

## Generation prompt
Original ceramic humanoid steward, a systems steward companion, measured, warm, deliberate presence, tactile material from the parent family, legible joints, believable material thickness, quiet architectural lighting, one clear silhouette, plain neutral study background, no text or UI. Use the authorized family reference and retain its source hash. Generate one subject with front/side/back views as separate renders.

## UI placement
Use 32px identity glyph for dense lists; 64px portrait in an inspector; 256px study in onboarding. Full family art belongs to an optional discovery panel. Controls and text sit on opaque backgrounds.

## Motion
A restrained gaze can orient a deliberate selection. Status is shown with a separate labelled indicator from verified runtime events. No ambient animation in reduced-motion mode; use a still pose.

## Forbidden drift
Do not introduce vendor marks, copyrighted mascots, new horns, face replacements or unrecorded color mutations. A material change becomes a versioned edition, not a silent replacement.
