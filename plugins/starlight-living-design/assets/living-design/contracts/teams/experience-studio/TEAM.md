# Experience studio

Aureon frames the mission, Nacre maps dependencies, Cyra composes the journey, and Orin reviews the exact result. These are design roles, not four running workers. One accountable operator owns the handoff.

Keep role, character, assigned worker, model route and execution instance separate. A sample team costs nothing to render. Invoke additional workers only when the job needs them and the current host authorizes delegation.
