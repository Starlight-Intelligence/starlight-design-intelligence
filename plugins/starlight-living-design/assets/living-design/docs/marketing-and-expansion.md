# Marketing and expansion

Version 1 · 29 October 2026 · proposed product positioning and experiments.

## Product thesis

**Make your agent team recognizable, understandable, and useful.**

Starlight Living Design connects visual identity to professional roles, behavior, interface states, and capability evidence. The initial audience is AI product designers, technical founders, and teams building agent workspaces or companion experiences. The deliverable is a portable design and workflow package. It does not sell the existence of a live collective.

The mechanism is concrete: start from an inspected reference; define character, design, role, values, and personality; map skills and connectors; produce component states and an inspectable team view. A successful first use leaves the builder with an editable identity package and a useful interface specification they can implement.

## Demonstrate the work

| Surface | Candidate message | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | “Give every role a recognizable presence.” | One character paired with its functional role and an inspectable example output |
| Product walkthrough | “From reference image to a complete agent identity.” | Actual source contracts, validation result, and generated projection |
| Team view | “See the people, agents, and capabilities behind the result.” | Accurate responsibilities and source links; live claims require runtime evidence |
| Educational content | “Design the failure state as carefully as the welcome.” | A visible unavailable-source or recovery flow |
| Community | “Contribute a character that helps someone do better work.” | A reviewed contribution with provenance, role, and reusable component examples |

Keep proposed copy separate from published claims. There are no measured conversion, retention, revenue, or customer-satisfaction results in this release. Current design hypotheses should be tested against the existing interface, not advertised as established gains.

## Distinctive product extensions

**Character passports.** Show lineage, materials, recognition anchors, profession, voice, rights/provenance state, compatible surfaces, and version history. Let users inspect why a character looks and behaves as it does.

**Role wardrobes.** Preserve one character's identity across a compact Operator avatar, an Academy mentor, and an expressive Cultural scene. This gives the brand range without maintaining unrelated mascots for every page.

**Team recipes.** Offer editable research, creation, learning, and review compositions. Each recipe explains responsibilities, handoffs, required tools, and useful output. Recipes remain blueprints until a runtime is connected.

**Evidence-led recognition.** Show completed artifacts and accepted contributions in a character's history. Base progress on real work; avoid invented competence levels, anthropomorphic distress, or paid rarity as a substitute for usefulness.

**Design compatibility packs.** Compile the same identity contracts into Figma metadata, application tokens, documentation, and image prompts. Treat exports as source-pinned projections with compatibility checks.

## Grow the cast through acceptance

| Stage | Candidate scope | Acceptance before expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Four families and twelve character recipes | References inspectable; roles distinct; contracts validate; useful sample journey |
| Pilot | Three characters in one complete product task | Real first-use/recovery tasks; role recognition; artifact usefulness; accessibility |
| Collection | A small reviewed set of professions and editions | Low identity confusion; maintained source lineage; consistent component behavior |
| Contributor network | Hundreds of proposals in a versioned catalog | Submission/review workload measured; rights/provenance complete; duplicates controlled; clear ownership |

Treat the stage sizes as planning scope, not adoption claims. Admit a new character when it adds a distinct audience, profession, interaction, or authored world contribution. Use editions for material/color variation. Reuse existing roles and agents whenever possible.

## Measurement and economics

Measure role-matching accuracy, time to the first useful artifact, task completion, error recovery, return-use recognition, accessibility defects, and maintenance effort. Compare the character treatment with the current product experience on the same task. Record cohort, period, denominator, and exact design/runtime version.

Count reference preparation, generation retries, editing, review, export, storage, integration, and maintenance in cost per accepted design. Model/tool spend is unknown unless a receipt exists. Pricing, a paid catalog, licensing scope, and community incentives require separate product decisions; this package does not create a price or checkout flow.

The strongest early proof is one finished task that users understand and complete more easily. Scale the cast after that result is observed.
