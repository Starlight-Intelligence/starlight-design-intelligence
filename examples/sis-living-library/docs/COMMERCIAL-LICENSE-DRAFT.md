## Delivery, updates and support

The purchase covers the exact edition and artifact version in the order confirmation. Proposed included service: access to compatible fixes/minor updates released for that edition for 12 months, and installation/reproducible-defect support for 30 calendar days after purchase. An update schedule, new major versions, custom development, accessibility certification, model credits, deployment, hosting and third-party support are excluded. Existing acquired-source rights do not end when the update window ends.

No blanket claim that downloading automatically removes a withdrawal right is made. After a full refund, access to future downloads/updates is revoked and use of refunded source must stop, subject to applicable law and any end-product treatment approved in the final terms.
